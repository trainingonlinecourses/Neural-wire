About this desk

Every headline, model, repo and radar signal is fetched live — and every successful fetch is persisted to your browser, so the desk loads instantly on revisit and keeps showing real last-fetched data even when a wire (or your network) is down. Fresh syncs refresh cached data in place.

Auto wire sync every 3 min · Persistent cache for news/GitHub/HF/spotlight · Unread history + filters persist · Popup-safe links + clipboard fallback · Press ? for shortcuts.
